Brain × AI 101

A Neuroscience-AI Curriculum Toolkit for High School AI Education

Brain × AI 101 is an NSF-funded initiative bringing neuroscience and artificial intelligence to life for high school students nationwide. Through a guided interactive experience, learners move from biological neuron signals to artificial neural networks, CNN filters, and feedback-driven learning.

Curriculum

Three progressive modules

The guided course moves from biological neurons to artificial networks, then explores how intelligent systems adapt through feedback.

  1. 1

    Meet the Neuron

    Biological neural networks

    • Synapses and activation
    • Signal transmission
    • Interactive simulations
  2. 2

    Seeing & Thinking

    From biological to artificial neurons

    • Artificial neurons
    • Deep learning
    • Computer vision lab
  3. 3

    Learning to Learn

    How systems adapt through feedback

    • Feedback loops and backpropagation
    • Clustering and reinforcement learning
    • Adaptation and intelligence

Learning experience

Built for active exploration

Rather than reading a digital textbook, students manipulate signals, layers, kernels, clustering points, rewards, and feedback loops. The classroom-friendly course includes an optional pre-course evaluation, three interactive modules, a post-course feedback and knowledge check, and a completion page.

Open access

Designed for broad reach

The project partners with the University of Florida CPET and SSTP to extend its reach to Florida school districts and build a replicable model for university–K–12 partnerships nationwide. All resources will be open access through GitHub Education, OER Commons, EDUCAUSE, MERLOT, CSTA, and the AI4K12 initiative.
